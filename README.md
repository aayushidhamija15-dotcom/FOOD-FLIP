🍕 Food Flip 🥗
No compromise on taste, no compromise on health — just Flip your food! 💚
Food Flip is a website that gives your favorite foods a healthy twist.
It helps you discover healthier alternatives to popular foods like pizza and burgers, so you can enjoy great taste while making healthier choices. ✨
🌱 Our Goal
To bring taste and health together without compromising on either.

HTML
CSS
JavaScript
❤️ Features
Healthy alternatives to popular foods
Simple and user-friendly interface
Delicious and healthier food options
Focus on both taste and health
